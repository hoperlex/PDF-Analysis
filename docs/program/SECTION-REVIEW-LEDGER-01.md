# SECTION-REVIEW-LEDGER-01 — the ledger of the 14 section decisions: what `reviewed` means and what nobody checked

Task: `docs/program/tasks/SECTION-REVIEW-LEDGER-01.md`. Written 2026-10-26 on the tip `2d8fa1392f46eea06b964aeaa419b6e3b2d2a390` of the branch integration/ar-close-1026. A document only: it reads the 14 section reports and the tree, and changes no profile, prompt, test or fixture.

**`reviewed` here means a lane wrote a decision for every open question of the section's record; nobody, neither an expert nor the owner, reviewed it (`R-146ag`, `A-7`).**

## What the table is

The owner's ruling `R-146ag` (`docs/program/OWNER_RULINGS_2026-09-17.md`, section 3.46) took the 14 non-AR section profiles from `draft` to `reviewed` with no human review: «Дописать и сразу перевести в reviewed без приёмки». A section leaves `draft` when a lane has answered every open question of its record with a written decision, rebuilt the section's recording and the expectations line says `reviewed`. The ruling's own wording of the accepted risk `A-7`: the status `reviewed` on these 14 profiles carries no human review; it must not be read as expert verification anywhere (reports, release notes, the ledger of decisions). This is that ledger. AR has no package (its profile is builtin) and is not in it.

Each lane wrote one report, named SECTION-<CODE>-FULL-01.md and kept in docs/program, and the table has one row per section, in the registry order `AI PS POS TX KJ KM OV VK PT EOM SS ITP GP PB`. The decision vocabulary is the dispatch's (`docs/program/dispatch/PRODUCTION-WAVE-PW2B.md`): **K** keeps the v1 behaviour, **N** narrows the prompt (wording that removes a stated false-positive source, never one that widens), **X** excludes an item.

How each column was taken (the commands and their output are in the task file):

- `profile_version`, status and title: read from the profile.json of each package. All 14 rows read `reviewed` at `1.1.0`.
- K / N / X: every row of the decision tables under «Decisions» of the report, counted once; a question the template asks in two halves has two rows. The cell also splits the rows into the section's own questions, the lane's designation audit (a check the template does not name: every numeral and class designation of the prompt against the pinned source) and the four cross-section questions that the wave table fixes for every lane.
- Prompt changes: the difference between the system_prompt.md at its draft commit (`e027b0d9`; for PB `bc574621`) and the tree, one sentence per change. Every quoted fragment was checked mechanically: after «adds» it occurs in the current prompt and not in the draft; after «removes» it occurs in the draft and not in the current prompt. Every difference between a draft prompt and the current one falls inside one of the listed changes.
- Assumptions: the list «Assumptions an expert would still want to check» of the report (for PS «Limits and what a domain expert would still check»), copied word for word and numbered by the ledger (the KJ report uses bullets). The only edit: the backticks around the names checklist.md and project_params.md were removed, because those are files of the legacy repository and not paths of this one. The reports introduce the list with the sentence «none of these was checked by anyone with domain knowledge».
