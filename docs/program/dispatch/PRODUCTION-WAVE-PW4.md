## 6. Lanes G1, G2, G3 — `SECTION-WIDEN-G<n>-01` (`R-146an`; `A-5`, `A-7`)

Branches `agent/section-widen-g<n>-01`, worktrees `.local/worktrees/section-widen-g<n>-01`. Groups: **G1** `AI PS POS TX KJ`; **G2** `KM OV VK PT`; **G3** `EOM SS ITP GP` (`PB` has nothing reinstatable: the gap report and `open_questions.md` question 10 — no lane).

**Result.** For every section of the group, the prompt asks for the same-document checks that the gap report marks «reinstatable now» in section 2.3 (read the row of the section in `docs/program/ROADMAP-GAP-2026-10-27.md`, the column «Reinstatable now», and the section's record in `docs/program/W59-SECTION-PROFILES-01.md` for the exact excluded item and open question it comes from). The topics: a total against the sum of its rows; a total against 100 % (POS distribution, GP land balance); a building or system total against the sum over its parts (OV, VK, PT: jets × single-jet flow, ВПВ + АУПТ); the same quantity stated in two units (PT: л/с ↔ м³/ч, ITP: Гкал/ч ↔ кВт ↔ МВт); an item named on a scheme/plan/explication and absent from the specification/schedule, and the reverse (AI, TX, EOM, SS, ITP); a requested value against the selected one (ITP); a capacity against a count (SS). **Every one of them is reported in the existing category `internal_contradiction`** with the existing evidence (quotes of the places that disagree; for an omission, the quote of the place that names the item plus the quote of the heading or row range where it should appear — if the schema cannot carry that honestly, the lane decides **X** for the omission topic and says why). The lane adds no category, no field, no severity, no norm.

**Conservative rules (decided by the integrator; there is no expert, `A-7`).** (1) Flag only when both figures are written explicitly in the text and the difference exceeds the rounding of the less precise figure (state the tolerance rule in the prompt in words, not a magic number). (2) Never recompute from a figure the text does not state; never use a value from a drawing, a picture or an OCR guess. (3) Where the section's record names a legal overlap or a stated basis (duration sums with overlapping stages; mass with or without weld metal; heat load «с учётом / без учёта» of a stated item), the prompt says not to flag unless the text states the basis. (4) Physical unit-conversion factors that are definitions, not norms (1 м³/ч = 3.6 · 1 л/с ⇒ 1 л/с = 3.6 м³/ч; 1 Гкал/ч = 1.163 МВт = 1163 кВт), may appear in the prompt and go into `prompt_token_allowlist.json` with a reason; any other number, norm reference, threshold or class designation not in the section's hash-pinned legacy source is still forbidden (`SECTION-PROMPT-TOKEN-GUARD-01`). (5) Notations that the record says experts treat as one value but the text may mean differently (`150/70 со срезкой на 130` vs `130/70`; `С345` vs `09Г2С`; `REI 60` vs `EI 60`) stay **excluded** unless a hash-pinned legacy source states the equivalence. (6) Each topic gets a decision (the existing vocabulary K/N/X plus **W — widen** for these topics) in a table; a topic the lane cannot make conservative is **X** with the reason.

**Steps.** Per section: edit `system_prompt.md` (keep the v1 skeleton that `tests/unit/analysis/text_profiles/test_text_profile_packages.py` reads); bump `profile_version` to the next minor and keep `status` `reviewed`; add to the section's fixtures one **positive** recording (a text in which a total differs from its rows → the recorded reply has a finding with the two quotes) and one **negative** (consistent totals → no finding), through `fixtures/recorded/sections/build_fixtures.py` (read `fixtures/recorded/sections/README.md` first; editing a prompt orphans its recording; run `build_fixtures.py --check` last); regenerate the section's line of `tests/unit/analysis/text_profiles/section_profile_expectations.json` from `profile.json` (the file is one line per section; conflicts with other lanes are resolved by the integrator); add the narrowing/widening sentences as **fragments** in the report table (section, added fragment verbatim) so that `SECTION-PROMPT-SENTENCE-GUARD-01` can read them.

**Allowed paths.** `src/auditmanager/analysis/text/profiles/<code>/**` and `fixtures/recorded/sections/<code>/**` for the codes of the group only, the section's line of the expectations file, `tests/unit/analysis/text_profiles/prompt_token_allowlist.json` (additions with a reason, append-only), `docs/program/tasks/SECTION-WIDEN-G<n>-01.md`, `docs/program/SECTION-WIDEN-G<n>-01.md`. **Forbidden:** the other groups' files, `W59-SECTION-PROFILES-01.md`, `SECTION-REVIEW-LEDGER-01.md`, the registry, `prompt.py`/`response.py`/`stage.py` (the categories stay the two constants), `P02_LOCK.json`, `contracts/**`, migrations, the web, `infra/**`.
