# AR-V3-LIVENESS-01 — a model call lives while its session shows signs of life

Written 2026-10-29 by the integrator from the final reports of two Sonnet lanes: `AR-V3-LIVENESS-WRAPPER-01` (branch `agent/ar-v3-liveness-wrapper-01`, tip `009ab1de`) and `AR-V3-LIVENESS-STAND-01` (branch `agent/ar-v3-liveness-stand-01`, tip `71a2efe5`), both on base `710a5eb1`; merged as `8336e687`. The lanes cannot create `.md` files, so this report is the integrator's. Task file: `docs/program/tasks/AR-V3-LIVENESS-01.md`.

## Why

After the wire-schema (`D-280`) and output-cap (`D-284`) fixes the owner switched the live stand to connections (`AUDITMANAGER_PROVIDER_SOURCE=database`) and routed «Анализ текста» to `kzSonnet`, an `ssh_command` connection that runs Claude Code headless on the development server. Three Runs (F1, F2, A1) were cut at exactly 780 s and ended `dependency_unavailable`: the call's timeout is the connection's cut to what remains of the 900 s Attempt watchdog minus a 60 s margin, and the command's own deadline was 840 s. A tiny call through the same flags took 2.8 s, so nothing was unavailable: the model was still working. Every limit on the path was one short wall clock, so a model that is thinking was indistinguishable from one that hangs. The owner's rule (2026-10-29): do not limit the model's thinking; check that the session is alive and extend the limits automatically; ceiling 2 hours, silence window 10 minutes, no interim release.

## What changed

**The command** (`tools/executor-runner/claude_answer.py`, lane A): Claude Code is started with `--output-format stream-json --verbose --include-partial-messages` and its stream file is read incrementally; the last `result` line is the report and every existing check (exactly one model in `modelUsage` equal to the requested one, no permission denial, success subtype, completed terminal reason, `structured_output` an object) is unchanged. In `--ssh-envelope` mode it writes `pdf-analysis-forced-command: hb <elapsed> <events> <output_tokens>` to stderr at start and every 30 s; it stops its own CLI after 600 s without a new stream byte (`claude_stalled`) or at 7140 s (`claude_timeout`); the old 840 s is gone. The task may carry `effort` (`low medium high xhigh max`, anything else `invalid_effort`), passed as `--effort`; absent means no flag; no thinking, turn or budget cap is ever set. A hung-up stand (stderr write error) kills the CLI's process group. The pull path keeps its 510 s deadline and sends no heartbeat.

**The stand** (lane B): `jobs/lease.py` — `ATTEMPT_WATCHDOG_SECONDS = 900` stays for an Attempt that never shows a sign of life; `ATTEMPT_HARD_CAP_SECONDS = 7200` and `ATTEMPT_STALL_SECONDS = 600` govern a live one; `AttemptContext.progress()` makes it live, `remaining_seconds()` is `min(hard cap - elapsed, stall - since last sign)` when live; the lease heartbeat thread fences when that reaches zero. `executors/ssh_transport.run_command` reads stderr incrementally and recognises a sign of life only by `^pdf-analysis-forced-command: hb [0-9]+ [0-9]+ [0-9]+$` on a whole line; until the first one the call is bounded as before, after it by `live_hard_cap_s` and a silence of `live_stall_s`; outcomes `stalled` and `hard_cap` are failures with an uncertain effect. `ssh_adapter` passes the sealed request's `output_config.effort` (`high` for AR v3) and the live parameters and maps the two cuts to `dependency_unavailable` with `dependency` `executor_stalled` / `executor_hard_cap`. `jobs/temporaries.py` and `workers/service.py` key the temporary-file sweeps off the hard cap (a live attempt may now run 2 h). `jobs/public.py` re-exports the two new constants (the import guard allows cross-context imports only through `.public`).

Compatibility: the new command against the old stand (stderr lines are ignored) and the old command against the new stand (no heartbeat: the call stays on today's bounds) both work; the effort member is ignored by the old command. The sealed request, `request_sha256` and every digest are unchanged; the connection's `timeout_seconds` (1..3600) is unchanged and no longer bounds a heartbeat-capable call.
