## 0. The result in ten lines

1. **Wave table** (section 1): 59 items of the roadmap's section 4, from W56a to W64 and «after 1.0». Totals are in section 1.8.
2. **The 14 sections are delivered as routing, not as quality.** All 14 profiles are `reviewed` at `1.1.0` and every one asks the model one question and allows two categories, `internal_contradiction` and `explicit_placeholder` (`src/auditmanager/analysis/text/prompt.py:41`, applied to every package at `src/auditmanager/analysis/text/profile_registry.py:326`). The status carries no human review (`R-146ag`, `A-7`) and every recorded reply is synthetic and authored over the АР corpus, not over the section's own text (`fixtures/recorded/sections/README.md`). Nothing in the tree measures what any of them finds on real text.
3. **What the legacy profiles checked and these do not** (section 2): of 158 excluded items in the 13 legacy-ported records, 115 are excluded because a norm, a calculation, a judgement or a required-presence rule would be needed, 28 because the contract has no place for the finding (drawing-only evidence, legacy taxonomies), 9 because the other volume or an external document is not there, 4 are same-document checks that could be reinstated now, and 2 record a rule the profile already applies. The 4 plus the topics of the records' open questions (a total against the sum of its rows, the same quantity in two units, a scheme item absent from the specification) are the only part reinstatable without a contract change, and each is a widening that `R-146ag` forbade the lanes to make.
4. **Three workflow gaps that are code** (section 3): (a) a section run sends the whole text in one call and refuses a volume above 152,361 characters at the pinned model and the 1.00 USD ceiling, about 48 pages of the one real set's density, although the upload admits 500 pages; chunked analysis exists only as unconsumed primitives; (b) «Оптимизация проекта» (price matching and cheaper analogs) is hard-wired to АР (`src/auditmanager/optimisation/project_match.py:281`), so it is unavailable for the other 14 sections although vendor lists exist for all 15; (c) the version comparison serves proposals only: no approved sheet link is stored and the AI summary the owner chose (`C-2`) does not exist.
5. **АР has no expert-decidable finding.** The six-Run АР path is review-only: it writes no finding row (`src/auditmanager/runs/v3_review_terminal.py:67`), drops every accepted proposal without a norm citation (`src/auditmanager/runs/v3_review_artifact.py:101`), and the one function that would publish a grounded finding has no caller outside a test (`src/auditmanager/findings/citation_publication.py:230`). That is the consequence the owner accepted in `R-146g`; the decision, export, comparison and carry-over steps therefore exist for the 14 sections and not for АР.
6. **Prompts and schema** (section 4): the contract's closed `FindingCategory` list has 12 values; the section profiles use 2; `ar_v3` solicits 7 non-normative values (the 2 and 5 more) and 5 normative values and then discards all 7 non-normative ones. Letting the sections emit more is a sized option list (P0…P8), from "no contract change" (the existing `documentation` value and `proposed_severity`) to "contract, migration and a vision stage" (drawing evidence).
7. **Human or host** (section 5): the activation data of АР v3 (`D-204`), real documents and named experts (`OD-18`), the live model pin (`PROD-13`), the stand (norms import, TLS, roles, drills), the complete gate (`D-205`) and the owner's open choices — 27 items in one list.
8. **Recommended lanes** (section 6): five lanes that need no human decision and no host, with hotspots, sizes and a parallel plan; two of them change the contract and are sequenced.
9. **Two stale statements the owner might act on** (decided by the lane, listed in section 3): the dashboard sections panel and the upload operation's description both still describe the pre-W59 state.
10. **Not established** (section 7): quality of any section on real text, the live state of the stand, whether the complete gate would pass, and any calendar.

## 2. The 14 non-АР sections

### 2.1 What every section's analysis does today

One mechanism serves all 14. `startRun` reads the version's persisted, expert-confirmed section, finds that section's package in the registry and pins its identity pair (`src/auditmanager/analysis/text/profile_registry.py`, `src/auditmanager/sections/routing.py`). The package is two files, `profile.json` and `system_prompt.md` (PB adds `open_questions.md`). Nothing else differs between sections: the response schema, the user preamble, the page banner, the token limits and the two categories are v1's for all of them (`src/auditmanager/analysis/text/prompt.py:41`, `src/auditmanager/analysis/text/profile_registry.py:316-326`). The model receives the whole text layer in one call and answers one question: does the text give incompatible values for the same attribute of the same object in different places, or leave a literal placeholder? It may answer with `internal_contradiction` or `explicit_placeholder`, a Russian `finding_text` and `recommendation_text`, and one or more `{page_number, quote}` pieces of evidence; it proposes no severity, cites no norm and may not judge a decision. A quote that is not found verbatim on its page is not published (`src/auditmanager/findings/grounding.py`).

The section's own part of the prompt is three things: a block of 9 to 11 places to cross-check (a hint, «не третья категория»), the object kinds and two or three near-miss pairs inside conditions 1 and 2, and the list of norm families the model must not check. System prompts are 5,061 to 6,695 characters (read from the registry, `load_text_profile_registry()`); the v1 prompt is 3,012.

## 4. Prompts and the response schema

### 4.1 What the 14 profiles ask for and what they return

One request per run: the section's system prompt, the user preamble («Текстовый слой документа, N стр. …»), the text layer page by page under `=== Страница N ===` banners, `max_output_tokens` 16,000, effort `high`, adaptive thinking (`src/auditmanager/analysis/text/prompt.py`). One reply, a closed JSON object (`RESPONSE_SCHEMA`, `additionalProperties: false`):

```text
observations[]: category (internal_contradiction | explicit_placeholder),
                finding_text, recommendation_text (Russian prose, no verdict, no norm),
                evidence[] (page_number, quote)   -- one quote per diverging value, or the placeholder
```

There is no severity, no norm citation, no required or stated value, no block or region. A reply that does not parse element by element is dropped and counted; a truncated reply keeps its complete elements (`src/auditmanager/analysis/text/response.py`). An observation whose quote is not found verbatim on its page is stored as ungrounded and creates no finding (`src/auditmanager/findings/publication.py`, the `grounded` branch). The model's output is never the expert's decision.
